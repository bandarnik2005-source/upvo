Safety

Adults only. You stay in control.

Upvo is for finding training partners. We built it so you decide who you talk to and when.

  • 18+ only

    Upvo is for adults. You enter your date of birth when you sign up.

  • Chat opens on mutual consent

    Nobody can message you out of the blue. You send an invite first, and the chat opens only if the other person accepts.

  • Hide, block, report

    Hide your profile from the feed, hide your last-seen time, block anyone and report a message in a couple of taps.

  • Clear community rules

    Upvo is for finding workout partners. Explicit photos, harassment and pushy messages are not allowed. We review reports and can restrict or block accounts.

  • Delete your data any time

    You can delete your account yourself in Settings. We collect only what the app needs and never sell your data.

Tips for your first meetup

  • Meet in a public place, like a gym, court or park.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and when.
  • Agree on time and place in the chat beforehand.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, leave and report it.
